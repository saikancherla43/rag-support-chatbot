# Acme Cloud Pricing Plans

Acme Cloud offers three plans designed to grow with your team. All plans include unlimited projects, automatic HTTPS, and preview deployments.

## Starter — $0/month

The Starter plan is free forever and ideal for side projects and evaluation. It includes 3 team seats, 100 GB of bandwidth per month, 1,000 build minutes per month, and community support. Services sleep after 15 minutes of inactivity.

## Growth — $29/month per seat

Growth is our most popular plan for production workloads. It includes 25 team seats, 1 TB of bandwidth per month, 10,000 build minutes per month, autoscaling, zero-downtime deploys, and email support with a 4-hour response time during business hours. Services never sleep.

## Scale — Custom pricing

Scale is for organizations with advanced requirements. It includes unlimited seats, dedicated infrastructure, single sign-on (SSO/SAML), audit logs, a 99.99% uptime SLA, and a dedicated support engineer. Contact sales for a quote.

## Overage charges

Bandwidth overages are billed at $0.10 per GB on Growth. Build-minute overages are $0.005 per minute. You can set usage alerts in **Billing → Usage Alerts** to avoid surprises.

## Annual billing

Pay annually to save 20% on Growth seats. Scale contracts are annual by default.

## Changing plans

You can upgrade or downgrade at any time from **Billing → Plan**. Upgrades take effect immediately; downgrades take effect at the end of the billing cycle.
