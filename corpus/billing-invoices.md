# Billing & Invoices

How billing works on Acme Cloud: cycles, payment methods, invoices, and taxes.

## Billing cycle

Plans bill monthly on the anniversary of your signup date, or annually if you chose annual billing (20% discount on Growth seats). Usage-based overages — bandwidth at $0.10/GB and build minutes at $0.005/min — are billed in arrears at the end of each cycle.

## Payment methods

We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, and ACH transfers (ACH on Growth and Scale only). Add or update your card in **Billing → Payment Method**. Failed payments trigger three retries over 7 days before services are paused.

## Invoices and receipts

Every charge generates an invoice, available in **Billing → Invoices** as a PDF. Invoices include line items for seats, overages, and taxes. You can add your company's billing address and VAT number so they appear on every invoice.

## Taxes

US sales tax is applied based on your billing address. EU customers are charged VAT unless a valid VAT number is on file (reverse charge). If you are tax-exempt, upload your exemption certificate in **Billing → Tax Documents**.

## Refunds

Monthly plans can be cancelled anytime; you keep access until the end of the billing cycle and we do not prorate. Annual plans can be cancelled within 30 days of renewal for a full refund.

## Past-due accounts

If payment fails after three retries, services are paused but your data is retained for 30 days. Update your payment method to resume immediately — no data is lost during the grace period.
